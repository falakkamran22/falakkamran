My🥞BELEGIN WAFFLES😋

A warm and delicious breakfast treat with a crispy golden surface and a soft, fluffy center.
These easy waffles are lightly sweetened with honey and taste amazing with fresh fruit.

Receipe Detailes

  • Prep Time: 8 minutes
  • Cook Time: 12 minutes
  • Total Time: 20 minutes
  • Serves: 3–4 people/li>

Ingrdients

  1. 2 cups plain flour
  2. 1 tablespoon honey
  3. ¼ teaspoon salt
  4. 2 eggs
  5. 1 cup milk
  6. 3 tablespoons melted butter
  7. 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
  8. 2 tablespoons powdered sugar

🍓For Topping

  1. Fresh berries🍒
  2. Sliced bananas.
  3. Honey
  4. Powdered sugar

✨💫Serving Ideas

Serve these golden waffles warm with banana slices, berries, and
a drizzle of honey for a simple and tasty breakfast.
